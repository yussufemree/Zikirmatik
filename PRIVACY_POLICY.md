# Privacy Policy for Huzur Zikirmatik

**Last updated:** October 2026

Huzur Zikirmatik ("we", "our", or "the app") provides a digital dhikr & tasbeeh counter utility. This Privacy Policy informs users regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when using our mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
**We do not collect, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII).**
All user interactions, dhikr counts, custom targets, history stats, and app settings remain strictly on the user's local device.

## 2. Local Storage
The application utilizes device local storage (`SharedPreferences`) exclusively to:
- Remember current counter numbers
- Save user-defined custom dhikrs
- Store theme preferences, vibration settings, and sound toggles
- Maintain local daily streak statistics

This data is never sent to any external server or third-party service.

## 3. Permissions
The app only uses standard system capabilities:
- **Vibration/Haptic:** To provide physical tactile feedback upon tapping the counter.
- **Audio:** To trigger subtle click sound effects upon counting.

No camera, microphone, contact, or location permissions are requested or required.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The app contains no third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or behavioral advertising platforms.

## 5. Children’s Privacy
The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through your store developer profile.
